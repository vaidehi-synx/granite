Quarry Matrix Excavation

Generational stone cutters identify optimal mineral matrices direct at global quarry reserves. Precision drilling and hydraulic splitting isolate raw blocks with minimized physical stress.

Computerized Gangsaw Calibrating

Massive blocks are mounted on automated Italian multi-blade saws. A continuous stream of steel shot abrasives slices blocks into clean, uniform slabs with ultra-fine thickness tolerances.

[ STAGE 03 // REFLECTION ]

Diamond Auto-Grinding & Glossing

Slabs traverse a 24-head diamond grinding line. Successive grades of fine diamond abrasives strip away surface micro-cavities to unlock deep quartz reflectivity and rich color saturation.

Laser Inspection & Containment

High-accuracy optical sensors scan for structural integrity. Slabs are color-matched and loaded into reinforced A-frame wooden containment structures for direct ocean freight container dispatch.

Our Story

For over 25 years, Guru Granite has transformed raw geological history into permanent fine art, bypassing distributors to harvest unique mineral matrices directly from premium global reserves. By merging the ancestral wisdom of generational stone cutters with state-of-the-art Italian laser engineering, we achieve sub-millimeter structural perfection while enforcing a strict ecological blueprint of low-impact extraction and closed-loop water recycling.
